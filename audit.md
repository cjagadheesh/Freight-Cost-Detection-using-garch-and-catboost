# OceanPulse data and model audit

Audit date: 2026-11-22

## Audit result

The app runs its models and serves API results. Its market inputs are cached data attributed to Yahoo Finance, plus a user-supplied Koyfin export for the Baltic Dry Index (BDI). No mock market feed was found in the code.

The local Yahoo cache was compared with a fresh Yahoo Finance download for BDRY. Values matched through Nov. 18, 2026. The provider's Nov. 19 value differed slightly, and the download included a newer Nov. 20 close. The app's cached market data ends on Nov. 19, so it is real-source data but not fully current.

The independent API check found the forecast, model, backtest, market-data, and risk endpoints working. The allocation, timing, speed, landed-cost, and stress decision endpoints also returned successfully.

## Out-of-sample forecast results

The BDRY forecast is generated by fitted models, but its point predictions have little demonstrated edge over a random-walk baseline (predicting no change). Walk-forward skill was negative at every tested horizon. The 80% prediction intervals covered approximately 80% of outcomes overall.

| Horizon | Forecasts tested | RMSE | Skill vs. random walk | Direction correct |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 day | 1,421 | 3.78% | −0.46% | 49.7% |
| 5 days | 1,417 | 8.17% | −0.61% | 51.0% |
| 10 days | 1,412 | 12.11% | −1.87% | 48.2% |
| 21 days | 1,401 | 18.61% | −2.17% | 47.0% |
| 42 days | 1,380 | 25.43% | −0.35% | 50.5% |
| 63 days | 1,359 | 32.57% | −0.22% | 31.5% |

The BDI model's one-day backtest was stronger: 74.4% directional accuracy, 1.90% RMSE, and +26.9% skill versus a random walk over 134 forecasts. This covers only about one year. The five-day advantage was small and statistically inconclusive; the model did not beat the baseline at 10 or 21 days.

The latest supplied BDI print is Aug. 28, 2026. The app nowcasts the current level from BDRY and carries that uncertainty into its forecasts.

## Assumptions and illustrative inputs

The app identifies several decision inputs as planning assumptions: the sample fleet, approximate port limits and distances, vessel-class TCE ratios, and user-entered FOB coal prices. These affect planning calculations; they are not observed live market data.

## Freshness and limits

Accuracy figures are historical backtest results, not a guarantee of future performance. The raw-data manifest says Yahoo snapshots were fetched Nov. 19, 2026, and the model bundle was trained Nov. 21, 2026. Refresh is off at startup, so the app needs a data refresh and retrain to use newer market observations.

The local manifest and direct BDRY comparison support the source attribution and freshness assessment, but the Koyfin export is user-supplied and was not independently authenticated.
